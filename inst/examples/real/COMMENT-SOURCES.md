## Room associations

The Louvre's official pages associate the Mona Lisa with
[Salle des États, room 711, level 1](https://www.louvre.fr/decouvrir/le-palais/de-la-joconde-aux-noces-de-cana)
and the Venus de Milo with
[room 345, level 0](https://www.louvre.fr/decouvrir/le-palais/un-ideal-de-beaute-grecque).
The Malaysian Meanders account explicitly names the Galerie d'Apollon in the
passage used here. The associations follow these named rooms and the bundled
OpenStreetMap snapshot, rather than inferred visitor positions.

The snapshot contains two overlapping ways named Salle des États. Comments
are associated only with `osm-way-492611500`, whose description explicitly
names the Mona Lisa and the Wedding at Cana, to avoid duplicate comment icons.
The original geometry snapshot is preserved.
